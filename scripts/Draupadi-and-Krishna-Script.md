# "THE THREAD OF FAITH" — Draupadi & Krishna
### An Animated Mahabharata Short — Full Script

**Runtime:** ~9–10 minutes
**Style:** Rich 2D/2.5D Indian-painting look (Pattachitra / Rajput-miniature colors), golden light for Krishna, cold blues & greys for the Kaurava court.
**Language:** English (easily dubbed into Hindi / Tamil / Telugu / Malayalam)

---

| **Draupadi (Panchali)** | Dusky complexion, long dark hair, deep-red silk sari, fierce eyes | Strong, dignified; breaks only once — in prayer |
| **Krishna** | Blue-skinned, peacock feather, yellow pitambar, flute, gentle smile | Calm, warm, playful yet divine |
| **Yudhishthira** | Eldest Pandava, white & gold, crown | Soft, guilt-ridden |
| **Bhima** | Huge, mace on shoulder | Thunderous |

| **Karna** | Golden armour & earrings | Cold, bitter |

## SCENE 1 — OPENING: THE COSMIC THREAD
**EXT. NIGHT SKY — STARS**

*Soft tanpura drone. A single glowing golden thread floats across a starry sky, weaving itself into the shape of a peacock feather.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> There are threads that bind kings to thrones…
> threads that bind warriors to their vows…
> But there is one thread stronger than all of them —
> the thread between a devotee… and God.

*The feather dissolves into the title:*

**TITLE CARD: "THE THREAD OF FAITH"**

---

## SCENE 2 — THE CUT FINGER
**EXT. INDRAPRASTHA — RAJASUYA YAGNA — DAY**

*A grand palace of crystal and gold. Kings from every land. Fire altars blaze. Krishna is being honored first.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> In the glittering city of Indraprastha, the Pandavas held the great Rajasuya sacrifice. And the first honor was given to Krishna.

*SHISHUPALA, a king in black, rises and insults Krishna. (Keep brief — a montage of angry shouting, with numbers "98… 99… 100" glowing on screen.)*

**KRISHNA** *(calm, raising one finger)*
> I promised your mother I would forgive you a hundred times, Shishupala. That was the hundredth.

*The Sudarshan Chakra spins up from Krishna's finger — a blaze of light. CUT AWAY (no gore): Shishupala's shadow falls. The chakra returns — and grazes Krishna's finger. A drop of blood.*

*Gasps. Queens and servants run for cloth and medicine. Confusion.*

*DRAUPADI, without a moment's thought, TEARS the edge of her precious silk sari — RRRIP! — and wraps it around Krishna's finger.*

**DRAUPADI**
> Sakha, you're hurt.

*Krishna looks at the strip of red silk, then at her, deeply moved.*

**KRISHNA** *(softly)*
> You tore a royal sari for a small scratch, Krishnaa?

**DRAUPADI** *(smiling)*
> What is a sari compared to my friend?

**KRISHNA**
> Then hear me, Panchali. For every thread you gave today… I owe you a debt. And Krishna always repays his debts.

*Close-up: the red silk on his finger glows faintly gold. Music sting.*

---

## SCENE 3 — THE GAME OF DICE
**INT. HASTINAPUR — ROYAL ASSEMBLY HALL — DAY**

*Cold blue lighting. Huge pillars. Blind DHRITARASHTRA on the throne. BHISHMA, DRONA, KRIPA, VIDURA seated. DURYODHANA lounges. SHAKUNI rolls ivory dice in his palm.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> Jealous of the Pandavas' glory, Duryodhana and his uncle Shakuni invited Yudhishthira to a game of dice. A king could not refuse a challenge… and Shakuni's dice never lost.

**SHAKUNI** *(whispering to the dice)*
> Roll for me, my little friends…

*MONTAGE — fast cuts, a drum beating faster:*
- *Dice roll → gold coins vanish.*
- *Dice roll → chariots, elephants vanish.*
- *Dice roll → the kingdom of Indraprastha fades to ash on a map.*

**SHAKUNI**
> Won!… Won!… Won!

*Yudhishthira, sweating, stakes his brothers one by one.*

**YUDHISHTHIRA**
> Sahadeva… Nakula… Arjuna… Bhima…

*Each brother lowers his head and removes his crown.*

**YUDHISHTHIRA** *(barely a whisper)*
> …And myself.

**SHAKUNI** *(rolls)*
> Won!

*Silence. Then Shakuni leans in, smiling like a snake.*

**SHAKUNI**
> You still have one jewel left, Yudhishthira. Your queen… Draupadi.

*Bhima's fist cracks the floor. Arjuna's hand moves to his bow — Yudhishthira stops him with a look.*

**YUDHISHTHIRA** *(eyes closed)*
> …I stake Draupadi.

*Dice in slow motion — tumbling, tumbling — land.*

**SHAKUNI**
> WON!

**DURYODHANA** *(laughing, standing)*
> Bring her here! Let the queen of Indraprastha sweep the floors of my palace!

---

## SCENE 4 — DRAGGED INTO THE COURT
**INT. DRAUPADI'S CHAMBERS → CORRIDOR → ASSEMBLY HALL**

*Draupadi sits alone, praying before a small lamp. DUSHASANA bursts in.*

**DUSHASANA**
> Come, slave-woman. Your husbands have lost you!

**DRAUPADI** *(rising, furious)*
> How dare you! I am a queen. Ask my husband — did he lose himself first, or me? If he lost himself first, what right did a slave have to stake me?

*Dushasana grabs her by her long hair. She struggles. He drags her down the corridor. (Animate with shadows on the wall — keep it intense but not graphic.)*

*He throws her into the centre of the assembly hall. She falls, hair loose, then slowly RISES.*

**DRAUPADI** *(to the whole court, voice echoing)*
> Grandsire Bhishma! Guru Drona! Maharaj Dhritarashtra! You are the elders of this family — answer me! Is this dharma?

*She turns to each face. Camera pans:*
- *Bhishma lowers his eyes.*
- *Drona looks away.*
- *Dhritarashtra grips his throne, saying nothing.*

**BHISHMA** *(heavily)*
> The path of dharma is subtle, child… I cannot answer.

**VIDURA** *(standing)*
> Stop this! This is the destruction of the Kuru race!

*Nobody listens. Then young VIKARNA steps forward, trembling.*

**VIKARNA**
> She is right! Yudhishthira lost himself first. This wager is not lawful!

*A beat of hope… then KARNA rises.*

**KARNA** *(coldly)*
> Sit down, boy. A woman with five husbands has no honor to protect. Dushasana — take away the Pandavas' garments… and hers.

*The Pandavas silently remove their upper robes. Draupadi stands frozen.*

**DURYODHANA**
> Dushasana! Disrobe her!

---

## SCENE 5 — THE MIRACLE
**INT. ASSEMBLY HALL — CONTINUOUS**

*Music drops to silence. Only the sound of Draupadi's breath.*

*Dushasana grabs the end of her red sari and PULLS.*

*Draupadi clutches the sari with both hands, eyes darting to her husbands — they stare at the floor. To the elders — silence.*

**DRAUPADI** *(desperate whisper)*
> Arjuna… Bhima… someone…

*Dushasana pulls harder. She holds on with one hand and raises the other to the sky.*

**DRAUPADI**
> Krishna!… Govinda!…

*She is still holding the sari with one hand — still trying to save herself. Nothing happens.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)** *(softly)*
> As long as she held on to her own strength… she was alone.

*Dushasana yanks again. A long beat. Draupadi closes her eyes. Tears fall. And she LETS GO — raising BOTH hands, palms joined above her head.*

**DRAUPADI** *(full voice, echoing across the universe)*
> HE GOVINDA! HE GOPALA! HE DWARKANATHA!
> I have no one but You! Protect me, Krishna!

*CUT TO: DWARKA — Krishna on a golden swing, eyes suddenly opening. He smiles gently. The red silk strip from Scene 2 glows on his finger.*

**KRISHNA** *(whisper)*
> I am here, Krishnaa.

*CUT BACK — ASSEMBLY HALL. A wave of golden light flows from the sky into Draupadi's sari.*

*Dushasana pulls — and the sari keeps coming. Red… then gold… then saffron… green… blue… peacock-patterned… endless. Mountains of silk pile up on the floor.*

*Music: a huge swell of shehnai, conch shells, and choir chanting "Govinda… Govinda…"*

*Courtiers stand up in shock. Bhishma folds his hands, tears in his eyes. Vidura whispers "Narayana…"*

*Dushasana pulls, pulls, pulls — sweat pouring — his arms shaking — until he COLLAPSES on the mountain of cloth, exhausted.*

*Draupadi stands untouched, glowing, eyes still closed in prayer. A faint transparent image of Krishna stands behind her, smiling, flute in hand.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> She gave him one thread of silk. He returned it as an endless river of cloth. That is how God repays love.

---

## SCENE 6 — THE VOWS
**INT. ASSEMBLY HALL — MOMENTS LATER**

*Silence. BHIMA rises, eyes burning, mace in hand.*

**BHIMA** *(thundering)*
> Hear me, all of you! I, Bhima, swear — one day on the battlefield, I will tear open the chest of this Dushasana… and drink his blood! And Duryodhana — the thigh you slapped to insult her, I will SHATTER with my mace!

*Thunder rolls. Lightning flashes through the windows.*

*Draupadi slowly opens her eyes. She takes a lock of her loose hair in her hand.*

**DRAUPADI** *(quiet, deadly)*
> And I will not tie my hair again… until it is washed in Dushasana's blood.

*Jackals howl outside. Omens — crows circle, lamps flicker out.*

**DHRITARASHTRA** *(terrified)*
> Stop! Stop!… Draupadi, my daughter — forgive my sons. Ask for any boon!

**DRAUPADI** *(standing tall)*
> I ask only one thing: free my husbands. Let Yudhishthira be no one's slave.

**DHRITARASHTRA**
> Granted! Ask another!

**DRAUPADI**
> Free Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula and Sahadeva, with their weapons.

**DHRITARASHTRA**
> Granted! Ask a third!

**DRAUPADI** *(proudly)*
> No. Greed destroys dharma. A Kshatriya woman asks for no more than two. My husbands will win back the rest with their own strength.

*She turns and walks out of the hall. The five Pandavas follow her, heads high. The silk mountain glows behind them.*

---

## SCENE 7 — THE CONVERSATION
**EXT. RIVERBANK — DUSK**

*Peaceful. Orange sky. Lotus pond. Draupadi sits alone by the water. Footsteps — KRISHNA appears and sits beside her.*

**DRAUPADI** *(not looking at him, hurt)*
> You came, Krishna. But why so late? Why did you let me call, and call, and call?

**KRISHNA** *(gently)*
> Sakhi… when you called, you were holding your sari with one hand. You trusted your husbands, the elders, your own strength. I was waiting.
> The moment you raised both hands and said "I have no one but You" — I was already there.

*Draupadi turns to him, tears in her eyes.*

**DRAUPADI**
> And the cloth? Where did it all come from?

*Krishna smiles and holds up his finger — the small strip of red silk still tied there.*

**KRISHNA**
> From this. You tore your sari for me when no one asked you to. I only returned your love… with a little interest.

*Draupadi laughs through her tears. Krishna plays a soft melody on his flute.*

---

## SCENE 8 — CLOSING
**EXT. SKY — NIGHT**

*The camera rises from the riverbank into the stars. The golden thread from Scene 1 returns, now wrapping around the whole Earth.*

**NARRATOR (V.O.)**
> When the world turns its face away…
> when even those closest to us stay silent…
> remember Panchali.
> Let go of the rope you're holding… and raise both hands to Him.
> For He who wears the peacock feather never forgets
> even a single thread of love.

**ON-SCREEN TEXT:**
> *"Whoever offers Me with devotion a leaf, a flower, a fruit, or water — I accept it."*
> — Bhagavad Gita 9.26

*Fade out to the flute melody.*

**END CARD:** *"Hare Krishna 🙏 — Like • Share • Subscribe"*

## PRODUCTION NOTES

**Music cues**
- Scene 1, 8: Tanpura + bansuri (flute)
- Scene 3: Tabla / dhol building faster with each dice roll
- Scene 4: Low strings, heartbeat sound
- Scene 5: Total silence → explosion of conch, shehnai, choir "Govinda"
- Scene 7: Soft flute solo

**Color script**
- Krishna = gold & blue glow
- Kaurava court = cold steel blue, grey shadows
- Draupadi = deep red (her sari), turning to rainbow silk in the miracle

**Sensitivity**
- Keep the disrobing scene fully modest — the focus is on Draupadi's face, her prayer, and the endless flowing cloth, never on her body.
- Shishupala's death and the hair-dragging should be shown with shadows/silhouettes (family-friendly, YouTube-safe).
