The voices in this video are made with espeak-ng (an offline robotic voice).
To use real voices: record each line as a WAV file, name it exactly as in the list below,
put it in `custom_voices/`, then run `python3 scene.py && python3 score.py` and render again.
The lip-sync follows the new audio automatically.
